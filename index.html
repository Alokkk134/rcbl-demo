Manufacturing process

Seven steps from
tyre to fuel.

What happens to a batch of tyres, from loading the reactor to collecting the oil, carbon black and steel.

01 / FEED

Feed and seal

Waste tyres go into the reactor and the feeding door is sealed tight, with no gas leakage.

Sealed reactor
02 / HEAT

Heat the reactor

The reactor is heated with coal, charcoal, fuel gas, wood or fuel oil, and its temperature climbs slowly.

Flexible fuel input
03 / CRACK

Oil gas forms

At about 250°C the tyre rubber breaks down and oil gas starts to form.

≈ 250°C
04 / CONDENSE

Condense to liquid

The oil gas leaving the reactor passes through the condensing system and becomes liquid oil.

Liquid TPO
05 / RECOVER

Recover the leftover gas

Gas that won't liquefy at normal pressure goes back to the combustion system through a safety device.

Closed loop
06 / REUSE

Reuse it as fuel

That recovered gas helps heat the reactor, saving energy across the whole process.

Lower energy use
07 / DISCHARGE

Cool and collect

Once oil production finishes, the reactor cools. At 40°C, carbon black and steel wire are taken out.

≈ 40°C · Carbon black + steel
Nothing wasted

One tyre,
four outputs.

Every part of the tyre gets used. The oil is sold as fuel, the solids are recovered, and the leftover gas powers the reactor.

TPO ~45%
Carbon black ~33%
Steel ~12%
Gas ~10%

Pyrolysis oil

Three grades of industrial fuel oil. This is our core product.

Carbon black

Recovered from the reactor once it cools to 40°C.

Steel wire

The tyre's steel reinforcement, recovered after pyrolysis.

Recovered gas

Gas that won't condense is burned to heat the reactor.

Handling & safety

Safe from tank
to burner.

Safety data for everyone who stores, moves or burns TPO.

01Handling & storage
Stability

Stable under normal storage and handling conditions.

Incompatibility

Avoid contact with acids and oxidising agents.

Storage

Store in a tightly sealed container at ambient temperature in a well-ventilated area. Keep away from flames and heat.

Handling

Handle only in well-ventilated areas. Keep the container closed when not dispensing. Avoid skin contact. Use grounding and bonding devices when transferring.

02First aid
Eyes

Flush with eyewash solution or plenty of water.

Skin

Wash immediately with warm water and soap.

Inhalation

Move to fresh air. Get medical attention if symptoms occur.

Clothing

Remove contaminated clothing and wash it before reuse.

03Accidental release
Small spill

Contain and soak up with an oil absorbent. Don't use combustible material. Wear suitable PPE.

Large spill

Safely stop the leak and remove ignition sources. Keep runoff out of storm drains and ditches. Water fog can control vapours.
